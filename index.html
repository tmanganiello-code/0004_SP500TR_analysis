Basic Analysis of S&P 500 Returns

Weekly returns, January 1993 – September 2026

Tommaso Manganiello

The data

  • Index: S&P 500 Total Return (SP500TR), the S&P 500 with dividends reinvested.
  • Adjusted prices: the Adj Close column accounts for dividends, so returns reflect what an investor actually earns.
  • Timeframe: weekly. 1,757 prices from 25 Jan 1993 to 21 Sep 2026 give 1,756 weekly returns.
  • Method: return = percentage change of Adj Close from one week to the next.

Shaded: 95% of weekly returns fall between -4.64% and +4.74% (2.5th–97.5th percentile). The mean/volatility ratio (weekly Sharpe, no risk-free rate) is 0.096.

Annual returns since 1993

Calendar-year change in Adj Close. 1993 starts from 25 Jan; 2026 runs to 21 Sep.

Takeaways

  • The typical week gains about 0.2–0.3%, but a single week can swing from -19.8% to +13.3%.
  • The median is above the mean: a few very bad weeks pull the average down (negative skew).
  • Of 34 calendar years, 28 were positive; the worst was 2008 (-32.6%) and the best 1995 (+38.0%).
  • Losing streaks: 2000–2002, 2008 and 2022.
