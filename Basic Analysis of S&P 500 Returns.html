Basic Analysis of S&P 500 Returns

Weekly total-return data from 1993 to 2026: mean, median, standard error and annual returns

The data

  • Index: S&P 500 Total Return (SP500TR), the 500 largest US listed companies
  • Adjusted prices: the Adj Close column is corrected for dividends and splits, so returns reflect what an investor actually earned
  • Timeframe: weekly, from 25 Jan 1993 to 21 Sep 2026 (1,757 prices, 1,756 weekly returns)
  • Method: weekly return = percentage change of Adj Close between consecutive weeks; missing values forward-filled

The 95% confidence interval for the true mean weekly return is 0.12% to 0.34%: it excludes zero. The median sits above the mean, hinting at a few large negative weeks.

Annual returns since 1993

Annual return = compounded weekly returns within each calendar year. 1993 starts in late January and 2026 runs to 21 September.

Takeaways

  • 28 of 34 calendar years were positive; the 6 negative ones were 2000, 2001, 2002, 2008, 2018 and 2022
  • Best year: 1995 (+38.0%). Worst year: 2008 (-32.6%)
  • The average week gains about 0.23%, but with a 2.36% standard deviation, single weeks are dominated by noise
  • The narrow confidence interval comes from the large sample (1,756 weeks), not from low volatility
